Own your music across every screen — wrapped in Liquid Glass.

BitChord is a free, ad-free YouTube Music and local lossless audio player for Android and Windows. Enjoy hi-res FLAC/ALAC playback, DJ-style Automix, synced lyrics, and Listen Together rooms.

Audiophile-grade playback with a UI that stays out of the way.

Hi-Res Lossless & Stats for Nerds

Native FLAC/ALAC playback, USB DAC bit-perfect output, gapless crossfade, and real-time codec and sample-rate inspection.

🎧

DJ Automix & Synced Lyrics

Beat-matched tempo transitions and Apple Music-inspired word-synced lyrics.

Listen Together & Spotify Sync
